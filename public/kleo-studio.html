Kleo Studio – Hair Salon Brand & Packaging

UI/UX Design Project 2 (EGL312) · Nanyang Polytechnic · 2023

Deliverables
Persona, logo, product packaging, tote bag, flyer, loyalty card, Instagram stories
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Dimension, XD

The brand

Kleo Studio is a neighbourhood hair salon. The goal was a calm, premium identity that would make customers feel the salon understands their hair, and would extend naturally to a line of its own hair care products.

Who it’s for

The logo

The final emblem replaced a plain wordmark with a symbol that says “hair salon” at a glance and works on a bottle label as well as a shopping bag.

Packaging

Each product type has its own colour variant (for example, green and lavender) so customers can tell the range apart on the shelf, while the shared emblem and label layout keep it recognisably Kleo. 3D mockups were built in Adobe Dimension.

Print and social

Instagram stories

What I learned

  • A brand is tested by how far it stretches. The logo had to work on a bottle, a bag, a card and a phone screen, which is why the emblem replaced the wordmark.
  • Consistency comes from a system, not from repeating the logo: the same colours, type and layout rules tie the flyer to the Instagram stories.
